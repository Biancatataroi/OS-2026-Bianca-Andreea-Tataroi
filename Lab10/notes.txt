Operating Systems - Laboratory Work 10
Add Your Own System Call: trace

PART 3 - OBSERVATIONS

1. Why does trace 32 grep hello README show only read?

The mask 32 corresponds to 2^5, and system call
number 5 is read(). Therefore, only read()
system calls are displayed. Other calls such
as open() and close() are not traced because
their bits are not enabled in the mask.

2. Why do three different PIDs print lines
during the forkforkfork test?

In kernel/proc.c, the following line was added:

np->trace_mask = p->trace_mask;

This copies the tracing mask from the parent
process to its child. Therefore, all child
processes inherit the tracing configuration
and their fork() calls are displayed.

3. Why is the trace message printed between
"hi" and the newline?

The echo program calls write() twice.

The first write() prints "hi" (2 bytes).
After this call, the kernel prints the trace
message. The second write() prints the newline
character (1 byte).

Therefore, the trace output appears between
"hi" and the newline.

4. Which mask traces both open and close?

The system call numbers are:
open = 15
close = 21

The mask is calculated as:

2^15 + 2^21
= 32768 + 2097152
= 2129920

Therefore, the required mask is 2129920.
